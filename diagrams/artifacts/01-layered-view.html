Architecture Reference — Diagram 01

Layered View

AGENTIC DRUG DISCOVERY PLATFORM
L0–L3 · GOVERNANCE → ORCHESTRATION → WORKFORCE → INFRA
L0 — Human & Governance L1 — Orchestration L2 — Agent & Service Workforce L3 — Infrastructure (AgentENV)

Redrawn from the repository's Mermaid source. L0 holds the human review surface and everything that keeps a person accountable for what the system does. L1 is the single orchestration spine — every downstream call is either a StageTask handed to the agent workforce or a ToolInvocation handed to a model, lab, or simulation service. L2 is the working layer of agents and the services they call. L3 is the shared substrate — sandboxing, scheduling, and storage — that every block in L2 runs on top of.
