What the suite caught

The harness earned its keep on first contact: an observed-max fit bias (systematically potent-side), per-run ground-truth instability, confirmation labels that overrode the gate, non-hermetic tests, and a fresh-checkout crash. All fixed, all regressed-against.

Post-cutoff blind reproduction. Contamination-free by self-report and by never-published variant probes. Agent derived the pyrazine-carboxamide hypothesis from structures alone; blinded ranking ρ=+0.42 vs a trained RF's +0.08.

Multi-paper sweep · ChEMBL 2025
