Built on three principles

Most agent demos stop at talking. BioNexus is built so you can let it act.

01 · Safety first, and it checks molecules

The only path from an agent to a lab is the gateway: a policy engine the agent cannot modify or approve itself, budgets that cap consumption, versioned toxicophore screening of declared structures, and refusal of dual-use campaigns — recorded, not silent.

02 · Tiered trust

Deterministic rules hold the floor. AI may add a flag or demand human review — never clear a refusal. Citations are verified against PubMed: a hypothesis that fabricates a reference ranks below one that admits it has none.

03 · Everything auditable

Problems, hypotheses, decisions, submissions, results, verdicts — hash-chained. Replay any experiment with bn reproduce, seal the store with a Merkle root, export to your ELN.

Tested against real science

Blind reproductions of published papers, held out post-cutoff, with contamination probes that can actually fail:

AZ3246 / HPK1 · J. Med. Chem. 2025

Contamination-free by self-report and never-published variant probes. The agent derived the pyrazine-carboxamide hypothesis from structures alone; blinded ranking ρ = +0.42 vs a trained RF's +0.08.

Multi-paper sweep · ChEMBL 2025

Blind chemotype identification on 4/4 papers. And the honest part: no enrichment claim survives bootstrap CIs + BH correction at n=31–67 — reported as such.

Interoperable by design

Five interfaces, pluggable adapters: Opentrons, plate readers, SiLA 2, LC-MS, AlphaGenome Atlas, and Genentech's AssayLoop policies — their brain picks, our gates execute, the chain records.
