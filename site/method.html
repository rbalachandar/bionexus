Architecture

Five interfaces, pluggable adapters, one spine: everything is a contract, every contract is recorded.

The five core interfaces

The core depends on pydantic only. Every integration is an adapter behind one of these; the registry matches names to implementations at runtime.

The loop, drawn

Multi-round campaigns & policy slot

From round 2 an assaybench-style policy (e.g. Genentech's AssayFormer through the AssayLoopRanker bridge) ranks remaining candidates from provenance-derived observations — their brain picks, our gates dispose. Budget, diversity, safety and human approval still veto every pick.
