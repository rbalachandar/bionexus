Diversity and memory, in one pass

Round 1 selects by score with a one-per-family diversity cap. From round 2, a policy (ours or an AssayLoop ranker's) ranks only remaining untested candidates, from provenance-derived observations — and the flywheel has already discounted any family that failed before. Gates, budget and approval still veto every pick.
